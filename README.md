# NEMO Clothing Brand 👕
### Fashion That Speaks Your Identity

Welcome to NEMO, a Ugandan-based clothing brand inspired by culture, nature, fashion, and the feelings that make every person unique.

Our goal is to bring people closer to their identity through clothing that expresses personality, creativity, and individual style.

---

## 🌍 About NEMO

NEMO is a Ugandan-based clothing brand that brings together fashion, culture, nature, and personal expression.

We believe clothing is more than what you wear. It is a way to express who you are, what you believe in, and how you see the world.

Our mission is to create a brand that connects with people's inner feelings while celebrating creativity and individuality.

---

## ✨ Website Features

- **Home:** Introduces the NEMO brand with a stylish hero section.
- **About Us:** Shares the story, vision, and identity of NEMO.
- **Collections:** Showcases clothing and fashion inspiration.
- **Contact Us:** Allows customers to reach the brand.
- **WhatsApp Integration:** Makes it easier for customers to send enquiries.
- **Responsive Design:** Adapts to mobile phones, tablets, and computers.
- **Animations:** Adds smooth transitions and interactive effects.
- **Fashion Gallery:** Displays images that represent the NEMO style.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

This website is built using:

- **HTML5** — Website structure.
- **CSS3** — Styling, layouts, animations, and responsive design.
- **JavaScript** — Interactive features and website functionality.
